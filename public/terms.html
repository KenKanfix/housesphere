HouseSphere Terms of Use

Last updated: 24 November 2026

HouseSphere is a local-first household management app. Your household data is stored on your device and is not uploaded to a HouseSphere database. The optional AI assistant sends only the content you ask it to process to Google Gemini or OpenAI. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Subscriptions

HouseSphere Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription priced at US$2.99 in the United States. Prices in other regions are set by Google Play or the App Store. Payment is charged to your store account at confirmation of purchase.

The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Google Play or Apple App Store account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; no partial refunds are given for unused time.

Pro raises the built-in AI allowance from 3 to 10 actions per day on the device where the purchase is restored. Everything else in HouseSphere remains free, and you may use your own Gemini or OpenAI API key for unlimited AI actions without a subscription.

Free tier and daily limits

The free tier includes 3 built-in AI actions per calendar day. Limits are enforced on the server and reset every 24 hours. Fair-use limits may be adjusted to reflect AI provider costs; existing subscribers keep the Pro allowance for the duration of their paid period.

Restoring purchases

Use Settings → Restore purchases after reinstalling or changing devices. Purchases are tied to the anonymous install identifier generated on the device; clearing all app data or uninstalling without restoring may prevent automatic recovery.

Availability and changes

The AI assistant depends on third-party services and may be unavailable from time to time. HouseSphere is provided "as is". We may update these terms; the current version is always available at this address.
