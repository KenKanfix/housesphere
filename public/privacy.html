HouseSphere

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Your household data stays on your device Inventory, photos, receipts, documents, expenses and bills are stored locally in your browser’s IndexedDB.
No account, no cloud database HouseSphere does not require sign-up and does not sync your data to a server we operate.
AI features are optional The assistant and scanning send content to the configured AI provider only when you choose to use them.
No tracking or advertising There are no analytics or ad SDKs, and we do not sell or share your household data.

1. Who we are

HouseSphere (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a household management application available as a website, an installable progressive web app (PWA) and an Android app delivered through a Trusted Web Activity (TWA). This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information stored on your device

The information you enter — such as rooms, inventory items, photos, receipts, warranty dates, maintenance tasks, projects, documents, expenses, bills, service providers and assistant chat history — is stored in your device’s browser storage (IndexedDB and local storage). This data is not uploaded to a HouseSphere database, because HouseSphere does not operate one.

Because the data lives on your device, it may be lost if you clear your browser data, reset the app or lose the device. Use the backup tools in Settings to export a copy you control.

3. AI assistant and photo scanning

The AI assistant and scanning features are optional. When you ask a question or scan a label, receipt, bill or document, the text and/or image you provide is sent to the app’s serverless API endpoint and forwarded to the AI provider configured for the deployment — currently Google Gemini or OpenAI — solely to generate a response or extract details.

  • API keys are stored server-side and are never exposed to your browser.
  • HouseSphere does not store your AI requests or images after the request completes.
  • The AI provider may process requests under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Only use scanning with documents you are comfortable sending to the AI provider.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy and OpenAI Privacy Policy.

4. Hosting and technical logs

The web version is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL and timestamps in order to deliver and secure the service. This information is used for hosting and security purposes and is not used by HouseSphere for advertising or profiling.

5. Backups and exports

When you export a backup, HouseSphere creates a JSON file on your device containing your app data, including photos and documents. You choose where to store or share that file. Keep backups somewhere safe, as they are not encrypted by the app.

6. Permissions

  • Camera / photos: requested only when you choose to take or upload a photo.
  • Notifications: optional, used for local reminders about maintenance, bills and warranties.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or background tracking is used by the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the app, or use Settings → Clear all data to erase everything stored on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser storage also removes the local data. AI requests are transient and are not retained by HouseSphere.

8. No sale or sharing of household data

We do not sell, rent or trade your household data. We do not use your data for advertising. The only third parties involved are the hosting provider (Vercel) and the AI provider you invoke when using the assistant or scanning.

9. Children’s privacy

HouseSphere is intended for adults managing a household and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the “last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be raised through the HouseSphere project page: github.com/KenKanfix/housesphere/issues .

This policy applies to the HouseSphere website, progressive web app and the Android app. The same local-first practices apply in the mobile versions.
